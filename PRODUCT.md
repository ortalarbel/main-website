## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript, statically generated. Chosen by the user from a recommendation (2026-09-28). Content lives in structured TypeScript files under `src/content/`, no CMS.

## Users

Primarily independent women (self-employed), creators and entrepreneurs in Israel, Hebrew-speaking, who have a strong inner critical voice and a tendency to perfectionism. They usually know what they want to do, but get stuck in procrastination, stress, over-effort and a life run by "צריך" (should). A secondary audience is women who dream of travelling solo but are held back by fear.

They arrive curious and slightly guarded. Most are on a phone, often from Instagram or Facebook. The job they are doing: working out whether Ortal understands their inner experience and whether one of her programs is the right next step.

## Product Purpose

Ortal Arbel's personal site, positioned as "אימון לביטוי עצמי מלא" (coaching for full self-expression). It has two jobs:
1. Build trust in Ortal as a person: her story, her worldview and how she works.
2. Move visitors toward a program: the free online workshop, the online course, or 1:1 coaching.

Success means a visitor feels understood, understands the offer and starts a conversation or registers.

## Positioning

Ortal's own story is the proof. She has had a strong critical voice all her life. Seven years ago she learned to identify it, separate herself from it and hold a dialogue with it. Since then she left a community she had lived in for 15 years, ended a decade-long relationship, travelled alone for nine months in South America and rebuilt her life.

Her key line is "הקול הביקורתי עדיין איתי. רק שהיום אני מנהלת איתו שיח, ולא כפופה לו." The critic is managed, not eliminated.

Her work combines deep emotional work and experiential exercises with practical tools. Sessions happen in her clinic in Moshav Ein Iron or online.

## Product Principles

1. The person before the product. Ortal's story and worldview earn the right to present programs.
2. Precision over promise. Name the reader's inner experience exactly; never promise outcomes.
3. The critic is managed, not defeated. Copy never claims to eliminate fear or self-criticism.
4. One clear next step at every point. The step is usually a warm conversation, not a checkout.
